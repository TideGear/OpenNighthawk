| S3 | The pull-out song did not stop when the cutscene was skipped (it did in the GOG release) | INVESTIGATING (not reproduced) | In the owner's session the skip was a mouse click; the game asks the driver for id 0 within 545 clocks, and the same happens for Esc, Space and Enter (125 clocks). The Roland driver then sends all-notes-off on every channel; Munt silences within a quarter second, offline and through the app's own audio path (`--audio-dump`). So the machine, the driver and Munt stop it. Suspect: audio playing late when the picture work overloaded the loop (R10); retest after the speed-up. |
| S4 | The recomp's sound effects are quieter than the GOG release (GOG: AdLib; the owner tried Mac and Roland; music and speech were fine; the briefing icons as they appear were the quietest) | CAUSE FOUND, fix built, awaiting the owner | All 43 sounds rendered on both drivers and measured (dBFS RMS, `D:/f117-gate/remaster-flight-20261031/all/`): Roland (Munt) is a median 6.2 dB quieter than AdLib over 36 sounds (peaks 2.7 dB lower), with a spread from 29 dB quieter (the red warning, id 9) to a few louder; Mac recordings are -10 to -26 dBFS with peaks near 0, louder than the AdLib sounds. Root cause: in `ON-roland` and `ON-mac` only the flight effects (ids 4 to 20, 23 to 25) came from the AdLib driver; the front end's effects (the briefing marks, id 28, the weapons page, 26 to 35) played from Roland through Munt. The hybrid rule is sounds: Mac then AdLib. Fix: every effect, ids 4 to 35, goes to the AdLib driver; stop (0) reaches both; the busy-poll (2), music (3, 36 to 39), release (40) and speech (41 to 45) stay with the loaded driver; a swallowed request returns AX = 0. Proof from the code: the only call sites that read the driver's answer are the three id 2 polls (START `30BF`, END `161A`, PLAYER `1D4`: `or ax, ax; jne` until 0), which is why swallowing id 2 once froze START. |
| S5 | The Mac sounds did not play at the hangar | PARTLY FIXED | The test copy predated the new mappings (intro music 3 to 200, weapons page 29 to 323 and 30 to 325, flight bay doors 31 to 323). The hangar door recording (Mac 324) has no DOS counterpart established: UNMATCHED. |
