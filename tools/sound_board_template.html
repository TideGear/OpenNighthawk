F-117A Sound Board

Every sound the game can make, played through each source: the three DOS sound drivers (AdLib, Roland MT-32 through Munt, PC speaker), the Macintosh recordings and the Amiga samples. Press a button to hear it; “All” plays a row's sources one after another. Mark each sound and pick the source you prefer; marks are saved with this page.

AdLib the game's ASOUND driver, rendered by DBOPL Roland RSOUND driver into Munt, MT-32 1.07 ROMs PC speaker ISOUND driver Macintosh the Mac port's own recordings Amiga the 8SVX samples

DOS sound ids

The ids the sound driver's table holds (0 to 45), each requested through the same entry the game uses. A sound that reached the five-second limit is marked 5s+; those are music or loops and carry on past what is shown. Ids with no sound in any driver are listed at the foot.

Speech

DOS keeps the nine words in one file, SPEECH.117, and chains them into calls. The clips are that file cut at its quiet gaps; the Macintosh has the same nine words as separate recordings.

Macintosh and Amiga only

Sounds with no DOS id in the table above.
